Which Annual Membership should I choose if I’m rejoining Inferno after a break?

Welcome back! Choose the Annual Membership for new members.

What happens after I purchase a Blackout plan?

You’ll receive a welcome email outlining your purchase details and terms.

How soon can I start using my membership?

You can start using your membership the same day you purchase it.

Can I purchase TNT Coaching without an Annual Membership?

No. TNT Coaching is an add-on to the Annual Membership and cannot be purchased on its own. An Annual Membership is required.

Does the Fire Pass have an expiration date?

Yes, all classes in your Fire Pass must be used by March 31, 2027.

Is there a purchase limit for Fire Passes?

You can purchase up to 2 Fire Passes per person.
